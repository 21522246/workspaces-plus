Move around

j k or ↓ ↑
Move the cursor down / up
l or →
Expand a workspace, then go to its first tab
h or ←
Go from a tab to its workspace, then collapse it
Enter or Space
Switch to the workspace or tab
1 … 9 0
Switch to workspace 1 … 10

Workspaces and tabs

n
New workspace, added at the end
d
Duplicate the workspace with its tabs
e
Rename the workspace, or edit the URL of the tab. Enter saves, click outside to cancel
r
Remove the tab. On a workspace, press it twice
J K or ⇧↓ ⇧↑
Move the workspace down / up

Filter

/
Filter tabs and workspaces: every word must be in the title, URL or workspace name
↓ ↑
Move the cursor while typing (also Ctrl+j k)
Enter
Keep the results, to use the other shortcuts on them. On an empty filter, clear it
l h
Show more / fewer tabs of a workspace: the matching ones, then all

Other

?
Show / hide this help (q also hides it)
Esc
Close the popup
Mouse
Click ▸ to expand, × to remove a tab, drag a workspace to reorder it
